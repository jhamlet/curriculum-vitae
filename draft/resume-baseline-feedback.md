# Baseline Resume Review Summary

Hiring manager critique feedback from iterative review process.

---

## Critical Issues

### 1. Employment Gap (2022-Present)

The 4-year gap is the single biggest concern. Current framing raises
questions:

- "Exploring the modern development landscape" is too vague
- Doesn't answer: What have you actually been doing?
- At senior level, gaps need specificity, not platitudes

**What HMs want to see:**

- Concrete activities (projects, consulting, OSS contributions)
- Evidence of current technical relevance
- External validation (shipped products, clients, adoption metrics)

### 2. Technology Stack Appears Dated

Listed technologies (RxJs, Ramda, Flow, Webpack) feel frozen at 2021.
Missing signals for 2026:

- Modern React patterns (Server Components, Suspense)
- Meta-frameworks (Next.js, Remix)
- Modern state management (Zustand, Jotai, TanStack Query)
- Current build tools (Vite, Turbopack)
- Modern testing (Playwright, Vitest)

### 3. Meta Descriptions Lack Specificity

Netflix section has strong metrics. Meta section is vague in comparison:

- "Identified common variations" — what was the outcome?
- "Mentored engineers" — how many? What did they ship?
- "Solo engineer proving value" — what was the value quantified?

---

## High Impact Suggestions

### Summary Section

- Lead with strongest hook (Netflix tooling story)
- Remove "27+ years" — invites age bias without adding value
- Add concrete capability statement, not tenure length

### Recent Focus Section

Consider removing entirely. Currently takes prime real estate to
explain you weren't working. Address gap in cover letter or interview
instead.

Alternatively, fill with concrete, verifiable activity.

### Netflix Section

The build system achievement is the strongest line:

> "40 seconds to near-zero"

This is memorable and demonstrates multiplier impact. Consider:

- Leading with this achievement
- Adding technical "how" (incremental compilation, dependency tracking)
- Quantifying "saving millions" with actual calculation

### Airtime Bullet

Current text is empty filler:

> "Social experience platform for web and desktop"

Either add meaningful accomplishment or remove entirely.

### Design Foundation Section

"Illustration," "Branding," "Marketing" seem off for engineering resume.
May signal generalist rather than specialist. Consider trimming to
engineering-relevant items only.

---

## What Would Push to "Definitely Interview"

1. **Address the gap with external validation** — consulting client,
   shipped product, OSS with adoption metrics

2. **Demonstrate current technical relevance** — not "I learned X" but
   "I shipped with X"

3. **Quantify Meta achievements** — specific patterns, tool names,
   measurable outcomes

4. **Add one recent professional engagement** — even small contract work
   proves current capability

---

## Bottom Line

Strong historical credentials (Netflix at scale, Meta). The gap is the
primary obstacle — not because it happened, but because the current
framing invites concern rather than confidence.

The fix requires real activity, not resume wordsmithing.
