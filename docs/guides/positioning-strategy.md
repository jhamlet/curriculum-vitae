**Created:** 2025-12-11
**Subject:** Jerry Hamlet - Career Pivot from Senior UI Engineer to Solution Finder
**Purpose:** Actionable guidance for resume rewrite, positioning, and market entry

---

## 1. Executive Summary

### The Pivot

**FROM:** Senior UI Engineer (technology-focused, implementation-oriented)
**TO:** Solution Finder leveraging AI as work amplifier (outcome-focused, strategic)

### Positioning Statement

> "I help teams ship faster by combining deep experience with modern AI tooling - taking on complex, cross-cutting problems that don't fit neatly into one team's backlog."

### Recommended Title

**Primary:** Creative Solutions Engineer
**LinkedIn/Search-Friendly:** AI Solutions Architect | Principal Engineer

### Critical Context

Jerry started AI-assisted development in mid-November 2025. This is NOT impostor syndrome - it is genuine capability without documented track record. The strategy must address this proof gap directly while leveraging 27+ years of foundational expertise.

### Key Differentiation

1. **"I am the training data"** - 27 years of experience is what makes AI useful; AI amplifies judgment, does not replace it
2. **Design as operating system** - Pattern recognition across domains, not just visual skills
3. **Spec-driven development** - Independent discovery of methodology GitHub open-sourced in September 2025
4. **Anti-performer positioning** - "Building, not performing" attracts substance-focused clients

### Target Market

- **Company Stage:** Series A-B startups (20-100 employees)
- **Industries:** Developer tools, productivity software, creative tools, AI/ML tooling
- **Rate:** $175-200/hr (with pathway to $250-350/hr for fractional leadership)
- **Engagement:** 3-6 month contracts, project-based with clear outcomes
- **Timeline:** Q1 2026 market entry

**What:** This resume/website refresh is being done with AI-augmented workflow.

**How to Document:**

**What:** Create a small tool or application that showcases AI-augmented development.

**Options:**

- MCP server for a specific use case
- Multi-agent workflow tool

- Solvable in 1-2 weeks of focused work
- Demonstrates both technical depth and AI integration
- Can be open-sourced for visibility
- Documents the development process alongside the output

**Why It Works:** Tangible artifact plus process documentation

#### 5. Principle-Based Case Studies

**What:** Frame past Netflix/Meta experience as timeless principles, applicable today.

**Examples:**

_"Scaling UI Internationalization"_

- Challenge: 1 country → 212 countries

- Application: How these principles apply to modern multi-market products

_"Building for Constrained Devices"_

- Challenge: Smart TVs and consoles with limited resources
- Principles: Performance budgets, graceful degradation, device-agnostic architecture
- Application: Edge computing, IoT, mobile-first development

_"Developer Experience That Scales"_

- Challenge: Build tools for 200+ engineers
- Principles: Developer time is the bottleneck; automation multiplies capability
- Application: AI-augmented developer workflows

**Format:** Blog posts or case study pages

**Why It Works:** Leverages existing credibility while demonstrating current thinking
