## Feedback Filter

When evaluating persona feedback on resume variants, apply these
filters:

**Does it align with objectives?**

- Does the feedback move toward $150-200/hr positioning?
- Does it strengthen appeal to Series A-B startups?
- Does it support contract/project engagement model?

**Does it serve target audience?**

- Would a startup CTO find this compelling?
- Does it address their pain points (cross-cutting problems, AI
  adoption, hiring velocity)?
- Does it demonstrate outcomes, not just capabilities?

**Is it actionable within constraints?**

- Does the suggestion require heavy on-camera performance? (reject)
- Does it conflict with "building, not performing" positioning? (modify)
- Does it lead with years in a way that risks ageism? (modify)
- Does it diminish design as "background"? (modify)

**Quality checks:**

- Is the feedback outcome-focused or technology-focused? (prefer
  outcomes)
- Does it strengthen the proof gap narrative or expose it? (must address
  honestly)
- Does it differentiate from generic AI consultants? (must
  differentiate)

**Red flags to reject:**

- Suggestions that commoditize (e.g., "AI Prompt Engineer")
- Feedback that emphasizes FAANG pedigree over principles
- Language that positions as pure technician without design-informed
  perspective
- Advice to lead with years of experience prominently
