## Conflicts Between Research and Current Personas

### 1. Culture Fit vs Culture Add

**Current Personas:** All three treat "culture fit" as a valid
evaluation criterion.

**Counter-Research:** "Culture fit" frequently masks bias against
underrepresented groups. Research shows hiring managers use "bonding
over rowing crew, scuba certification, single-malt scotch" as fit
evidence.

**Resolution:** Update personas to use "culture add" framing and
explicitly acknowledge culture fit bias in blindspots section.

### 2. FAANG Experience Interpretation

**Current Personas:** Treat FAANG experience neutrally or positively.

**Research Finding:** FAANG experience is double-edged:

- Engineering HM: Creates screening shortcuts (positive)
- Startup Founder: May signal "too corporate" (negative)
- Strategic Leader: May signal "operator" vs "builder" (negative)

**Resolution:** Add nuanced FAANG interpretation guidance to each
persona.

### 3. Reference Check Weight

**Current Personas:** Imply references matter.

**Counter-Research:** Reference checks have predictive validity of only
0.26. Favorable reports may reflect lenient referees, not candidate
quality.

**Resolution:** Downweight reference check guidance; focus on work
samples.

### 4. Resume Optimization Value

**Current Personas:** Assume resume quality is primary screening factor.

**Counter-Research:** "If you don't have top brands, better wording
won't help. If you do have top brands, the wording doesn't matter."
(Aline Lerner, interviewing.io)

**Resolution:** Add context about referrals and demonstrated work as
parallel paths.

## New Blindspots from Counter-Research

### For All Personas

1. **False Negative Blindness:** Companies optimize to avoid bad hires;
   rejected good candidates are invisible.

2. **Intuition Overconfidence:** Unstructured evaluation has 0.31
   predictive validity vs 0.62 for structured.

3. **Skills-Based Hiring Gap:** 85% claim skills-based hiring; fewer
   than 1 in 700 hires actually changed.

### Persona-Specific

**Startup Founder:**

- "Startup-ready" signals may exclude capable candidates from different
  backgrounds
- "Too corporate" may be coded language for "different from us"
- Speed pressure leads to pattern shortcuts that miss good candidates

**Engineering HM:**

- ATS blame is overblown; humans reject 75% of resumes, not algorithms
- Two-page resumes actually perform 2.9x better (contradicts common
  advice)
- Referrals are 4x more effective than applications

**Strategic Leader:**

- Experience-performance correlation is weak after 3-5 years
- "More experience can correlate with less startup output"
- Adaptability matters more than industry depth
