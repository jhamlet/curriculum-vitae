# Fractional.io Profile Optimization

**Created:** 2026-01-06

## Platform Context

Fractional.io connects fractional leaders (director+) with founders
seeking part-time strategic leadership. Different from LinkedIn:

| Aspect      | LinkedIn                | Fractional.io               |
| ----------- | ----------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Audience    | Recruiters, hiring mgrs | Founders seeking advisors   |
| Positioning | Job-seeker              | Advisory/leadership         |
| Rate model  | Hourly ($150-200)       | Monthly retainer ($10k-15k) |
| Tone        | Keyword optimization    | Outcome-focused             |

## Key Statistics

- 71% of CEOs vet fractional's public footprint before signing
- Leaders with visible expertise command 38% higher fees
- Typical market budget: $5K-15K/month

## Positioning Strategy

### Rate Decision

**Selected:** $10k-15k/month

Rationale:

- Anchors at target rate, room to negotiate up for larger scopes
- Filters price-sensitive clients early
- Gap addressed in narrative, not rate discount

### Framing Shift

| Before (Resume)     | After (Fractional)           |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| "I can do X"        | "I help companies achieve Y" |
| Technology skills   | Strategic outcomes           |
| Years of experience | Types of problems solved     |
| Available for hire  | Taking on select engagements |

### Timeline Gap Strategy

The 2022-2025 gap is addressed narratively as intentional reset, not
rate discount. Framing: "After a break to rest, reset, and recharge...
now energized by AI-augmented development."

## Trust Signal Hierarchy

### Tier 1 (Lead with these)

- Quantified outcomes: Netflix 212 countries, 100M+ users, Meta hundreds
  of engineers
- Strategic clarity: "problems that span teams"
- AI integration expertise (hot skill, premium positioning)

### Tier 2 (Supporting)

- Team scaling experience (Netflix 4→40 engineers)
- Cross-functional problem-solving
- Developer experience / tooling

### Tier 3 (Mention, don't lead)

- FAANG pedigree
- Years of experience
- Technical stack details
