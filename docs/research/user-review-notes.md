### What Doesn't Fit

- **Social media presence** — not interested, possibly averse
  - Question: Can being a-social be a differentiator?
  - Possible positioning: "Substance over social" / "Work speaks, not tweets"
  - Alternatives to social for discoverability:
    - Newsletter (owned audience, email-based)
    - Blog/RSS (old-school but respected in tech circles)
    - GitHub activity (code as communication)
    - Conference talks / podcasts (if occasional)
  - Being "off the grid" socially could align with clarity-over-noise positioning

- **"Building, not performing"** — core identity theme
  - Observed career pattern: performers get attention, builders do the work
  - Tension: Building an audience without performing is harder
  - But: The right clients *want* builders, not performers
  - Positioning opportunity: Explicitly contrast with "thought leaders" and influencers
  - Let the work be the proof — case studies, shipped products, tools that exist
  - This is a filter: attracts clients who value substance, repels those who want flash

## Direction Clarification

### Positioning Decisions

- **Core differentiator:** Solutions across complex systems
  - Finding elegant, synthesized solutions — not just solving one piece
  - Cross-domain thinking as a skill (not just multi-stack knowledge)
  - Balance: Must remain discoverable (SEO, keywords) while expressing this
  - Challenge: "Complex systems thinker" isn't a searchable term
  - Approach: Use discoverable keywords in structure, unique positioning in narrative

- **Agentic AI Development** — A focus, not THE focus
  - Rapid learning curve: Weeks to discover what community took months/years
  - Demonstrate practical application, not just theory
  - Position as: AI-augmented builder (AI amplifies, doesn't replace)
  - Avoid: Being pigeonholed as "the AI guy"

- **Design-Engineering Hybrid** — Probably the core strength
  - Design is NOT just visual
    - Marketing design
    - Workflow design
    - System design
    - Information architecture
    - Process design
  - "Design is everywhere, not just in front of your eyes"
  - Design = intentional structure, elegant solutions, considered trade-offs
  - This is meta-skill: design *thinking* applied to engineering problems
  - Differentiator: Most engineers don't think this way
