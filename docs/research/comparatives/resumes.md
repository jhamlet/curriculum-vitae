# Comparative Resume Analysis

**Research Date:** 2025-12-06
**Purpose:** Identify positioning strategies for creative solution architects, AI/agentic developers, and design-engineering hybrids
**For:** Jerry Hamlet - Career repositioning from "Senior UI Engineer" to "Creative Solutions Architect"

---

## Executive Summary

This analysis examines 10 professionals who successfully position themselves at the intersection of design, engineering, AI, and creative problem-solving. Key patterns emerge around:

1. **Title flexibility** - Most successful examples use non-traditional titles or multiple identities
2. **Mission-driven framing** - Leading with "why" and impact rather than job duties
3. **Multi-format presence** - Portfolio + blog + teaching creates authority
4. **Personality integration** - Approachability and authenticity differentiate
5. **Project-centric evidence** - Showing work trumps listing skills

## Cross-Cutting Patterns

### Positioning Strategies That Work

1. **Multi-Role Identity**
   - Most successful examples list 2-4 distinct roles/identities
   - Example: "Designer, anthropologist, and mediocre developer" (Maggie Appleton)
   - Creates memorable differentiation and shows range

2. **Mission Over Title**
   - Leading with purpose rather than job description
   - Example: "making the Web platform better for everyone" (Sara Soueidan)
   - Creates emotional connection and values alignment

3. **Honest Self-Assessment**
   - Acknowledging limitations builds trust
   - Example: "mediocre developer" (Appleton), "over-confident pair programming assistant" metaphor (Willison)
   - Differentiates from typical self-promotion

4. **Project-Centric Proof**
   - Tools, courses, or notable projects establish credibility
   - Examples: Tailwind CSS (Wathan), LLM CLI (Willison), Practical Accessibility course (Soueidan)
   - Shows impact rather than claiming skills

5. **Teaching as Authority**
   - Creating educational content establishes expertise
   - Multiple examples built courses, workshops, or wrote books
   - Teaching forces clear communication of complex topics

6. **Personality Integration**
   - Including personal interests and humor
   - Example: "BBQ Tips" (Bos), "meme-maker" (Williams)
   - Creates memorable, relatable positioning

### AI/Agentic Positioning Patterns

1. **Practical over Theoretical**
   - Simon Willison's approach: build tools, document learnings
   - Swyx's "AI Engineer" framing positions AI work as engineering discipline
   - Emphasis on what AI enables, not AI itself

2. **Balanced Critique**
   - Willison critiques "slop" while advocating AI productivity
   - Acknowledging limitations builds credibility
   - "They'll absolutely make mistakes" messaging

3. **Tool Building**
   - Creating tools that use AI (LLM CLI, Smol AI)
   - Demonstrates practical understanding beyond consumption
   - Creates proof of expertise

### Design-Engineering Hybrid Patterns

1. **Design-First Narrative**
   - Crediting design background for development approach (Silich)
   - "Works at intersection of design and code" (Soueidan)
   - Visual communication as differentiator (Appleton)

2. **Prototype Focus**
   - "Building early-stage prototypes" (Appleton at Normally)
   - Physical computing projects showing interdisciplinary range (Silich)
   - Emphasis on exploration over production

3. **Quality over Speed**
   - "Extremely detail-oriented, a bit of a perfectionist" (Silich)
   - Accessibility-first (Soueidan)
   - Positions as craftsperson, not just implementer

### Consulting/Independent Positioning Patterns

1. **Curated Client Lists**
   - Quality over quantity (Soueidan: Netflix, Khan Academy)
   - Emphasizes strategic partnerships
   - Selective positioning suggests expertise worth seeking

2. **Educational Products**
   - Courses, workshops, books as income + authority
   - Examples: Practical Accessibility, Tailwind UI
   - Scales expertise beyond hourly billing

3. **Multi-Format Presence**
   - Blog + podcast + speaking + newsletter
   - Creates multiple entry points for discovery
   - Builds omnipresence in niche

## Questions for Follow-Up

### For Jerry to Consider

1. **Identity Balance**: How to balance "Creative Solutions Architect" breadth with specific expertise areas? The most memorable positioning was narrow (accessibility, AI tools) not broad.

2. **AI Positioning Depth**: Is the goal to position as AI-native builder (like Willison) or AI-enabled creative (using AI as tool)? These require different evidence.

3. **Teaching Component**: Would creating educational content (courses, workshops, writing) strengthen positioning? Multiple examples leveraged teaching for authority.

4. **Physical Proof**: What tangible projects/tools can demonstrate "Creative Solutions Architect" capability? Most examples had specific, nameable work.

5. **Platform Strategy**: What platforms to prioritize for visibility? Blog, GitHub, portfolio site, social media each serve different purposes.

6. **Design Credential**: How to substantiate "design-informed" claim? Examples either had formal design background (Silich, Appleton) or design-focused projects (Soueidan).

### Research Gaps

1. **Senior IC examples**: Most examples were founders, educators, or advocates. Fewer pure senior IC examples found.

2. **Enterprise AI positioning**: Ragnar Pitla (Microsoft) profile not fully accessible. More enterprise AI architect examples would be valuable.

3. **Freelance transition**: Limited examples of transitioning from employee to independent consultant with AI focus.

4. **Resume format examples**: Most research focused on portfolio/personal site positioning rather than traditional resume format.

## Actionable Recommendations

### For Immediate Application

1. **Define 2-3 identity roles** that create memorable positioning
   - Example structure: "[Primary Role], [Secondary Role], and [Unexpected Element]"
   - Draft: "Solutions Architect, AI-augmented builder, and design-obsessed engineer"

2. **Lead with mission**, not job description
   - What problem does Jerry solve? For whom?
   - Example: "Building AI-powered solutions that bridge design vision and technical execution"

3. **Create proof projects**
   - Build 2-3 demonstrable AI-integrated tools or projects
   - Document process publicly (Learn in Public)

4. **Curate, don't list**
   - 3-5 high-impact projects > exhaustive history
   - Each project with clear context, challenge, solution, outcome

5. **Integrate personality**
   - What makes Jerry distinctive beyond skills?
   - Include without overwhelming professional positioning

### For Medium-Term Positioning

1. **Educational content** - even small-scale (blog posts, TILs)
2. **Open source contribution** - visible GitHub activity
3. **Speaking/visibility** - local meetups to start
4. **Newsletter/writing** - establish consistent voice
