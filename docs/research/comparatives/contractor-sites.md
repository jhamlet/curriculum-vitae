# Contractor/Consulting Website Comparative Analysis

**Research Date:** 2025-12-06
**Purpose:** Inform design and positioning for hamletink.com
**Target Positioning:** Creative Solutions Architect | AI Agentic Developer | Design + Engineering Hybrid

---

## Executive Summary

This analysis examines 10 contractor/consultant websites from professionals who position themselves at the intersection of AI, creative technology, and design-informed engineering. Key patterns emerge:

1. **Content-first design** dominates - minimalist aesthetics let expertise shine through writing
2. **Multi-revenue models** are common - courses, consulting, newsletter sponsorship, open-source tool sponsorship
3. **Proof through building** matters more than credential-listing - working tools and prolific writing establish credibility
4. **Soft CTAs** outperform aggressive marketing - permission-based engagement respects technical audiences
5. **Personal brand > agency positioning** - authenticity and personality differentiate solo consultants

## Cross-Site Patterns & Insights

### Common Design Patterns

1. **Minimalist Aesthetics**: 9/10 sites prioritize clean typography and whitespace over flashy visuals
2. **Dark/Light Mode**: 7/10 sites offer theme toggles
3. **Content-First Layout**: Blog/writing is primary navigation item
4. **Social Proof Integration**: Subscriber counts, download numbers, client logos

### Revenue Models Observed

| Model                   | Sites Using                                      |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Newsletter/Sponsorship  | swyx, Simon Willison, Eugene Yan                 |
| Premium Courses         | Josh Comeau, Hamel Husain, Jason Liu, Chip Huyen |
| Consulting/Advisory     | swyx, Hamel Husain, Jason Liu                    |
| Open Source Sponsorship | Simon Willison, Jason Liu                        |
| Angel Investing         | swyx, Eugene Yan, Jason Liu                      |
| Books                   | Chip Huyen                                       |

### Positioning Strategies

1. **The Educator**: Josh Comeau, Chip Huyen, Hamel Husain - teaching as primary value
2. **The Builder**: Simon Willison, Linus Lee, Jason Liu - tools/projects as proof
3. **The Curator**: swyx, Eugene Yan - synthesizing knowledge for community
4. **The Researcher**: Andy Matuschak, Maggie Appleton - exploring frontiers publicly
5. **The Craftsperson**: Brittany Chiang - quality and attention to detail

### Differentiated Offerings Worth Considering

1. **Digital Garden / Notes System** (Maggie Appleton, Simon Willison)

2. **Open Source Tools** (Simon Willison, Jason Liu, Linus Lee)

## Recommendations for hamletink.com

Based on this research, consider the following for Jerry Hamlet's positioning:

### High-Priority Elements

1. **Clear Value Proposition**: State the intersection of AI, creative solutions, and design-engineering upfront
2. **Proof Through Building**: Showcase tools, projects, or prototypes that demonstrate capability
3. **Writing/Blog Section**: Establish thought leadership through technical content
4. **Newsletter Signup**: Build audience for ongoing engagement
5. **"Start Here" or "About" Section**: Guide new visitors to understand positioning

### Differentiation Opportunities

1. **AI Agentic Development Focus**: Few consultants explicitly position around agentic systems
2. **Design-Engineering Hybrid**: The Maggie Appleton/Brittany Chiang intersection is underexplored in AI consulting
3. **Creative Solutions Framing**: Most consultants position technically; creative framing could differentiate
4. **Client Portal/Dashboard**: None of the analyzed sites offer this - could be unique value-add

### Visual Design Recommendations

1. **Modern Minimalism**: Follow the pattern of clean typography and whitespace
2. **Dark/Light Mode**: Table stakes for technical audiences
3. **Accent Color Strategy**: Pick 1-2 distinctive colors for brand recognition
4. **Custom Visual Elements**: Even simple illustrations or icons add personality
5. **Mobile-First Responsive**: All analyzed sites are responsive
