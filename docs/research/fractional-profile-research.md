# Fractional.io Profile Research

**Created:** 2026-01-06

## Executive Summary

Fractional.io is a community platform for fractional leaders (director+
level). Unlike LinkedIn (job-seeker positioning) or traditional resumes,
Fractional.io profiles target founders seeking part-time strategic
leadership.

**Key insight:** 71% of CEOs vet a fractional's public footprint before
signing, and leaders with visible expertise command fees up to 38%
higher.

## Platform Overview

### What is Fractional.io?

- Community where fractional leaders connect
- Member directory with public profiles
- "Verified Expert" tier requires interview/vetting
- Built on Softr (members.fractional.io/profile)
- Full-time team doing daily outreach to US companies needing director+
  talent

### Membership Tiers

| Tier     | Directory Visibility | Features                        |
| -------- | -------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Free     | No                   | Forum access, profile (private) |
| Paid     | Yes                  | Public directory, job alerts    |
| Verified | Yes + badge          | Interviewed/vetted, promoted    |

### Target Audience (Who Sees Your Profile)

- Non-technical founders at Series A-B startups
- CEOs needing fractional CTO/tech leadership
- Companies who "can't afford full-time salary" but need experienced
  leadership
- Hiring for: 5-20 hours/week engagements

## Profile Content Requirements

### Sections (Based on Platform Research)

| Section        | Content Focus                         |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Headline       | Title + Value prop (not just role)    |
| Bio/Summary    | Problems solved, outcomes delivered   |
| Expertise      | Strategic areas, not tech stack lists |
| Industries     | Specific verticals served             |
| Availability   | Hours/week, engagement models         |
| Rate indicator | Monthly retainer range (not hourly)   |
| Social proof   | Logos, testimonials, outcomes         |

## Positioning Strategy

### Key Difference: Advisory vs Job-Seeking

| Resume/LinkedIn           | Fractional.io                    |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| "I can do X"              | "I help companies achieve Y"     |
| Technology skills         | Strategic outcomes               |
| Years of experience       | Types of problems solved         |
| Job titles                | Value delivered                  |
| Hourly rate ($175-200)    | Monthly retainer ($5k+)          |
| Looking for opportunities | Available for select engagements |

### What Non-Technical Founders Need

From research on fractional CTO hiring:

1. **Clarity** - Can explain complex things simply
2. **Risk reduction** - Track record of shipping
3. **Outcome focus** - Business results, not tech bragging
4. **Availability** - Clear hours/week commitment
5. **Trust signals** - References from other founders

### Engagement Models to Highlight

| Hours/Week | Model Type             | Typical Work                     |
| ---------- | ---------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| 5 hrs      | Strategic-only         | Roadmap, decisions, hiring input |
| 10 hrs     | Strategic + management | Team oversight, execution        |
| 20 hrs     | Involved leadership    | Direction + hands-on as needed   |

## Trust Signal Hierarchy

### Tier 1 (Highest Impact)

- Quantified business outcomes from advisory work
- Testimonials from founders/CEOs (not just colleagues)
- Evidence of strategic clarity (writing, frameworks, talks)
- "Verified Expert" badge on Fractional.io

### Tier 2 (Strong)

- Industry-specific expertise
- Team scaling/hiring experience
- Technical decision-making track record
- Cross-functional collaboration evidence

### Tier 3 (Supporting)

- FAANG/prestigious company experience
- Technical credentials
- Years of experience
- Certifications

### What Feels Hollow (Avoid)

- Pure tech focus with no business context
- "I built X" without "which achieved Y"
- Buzzword lists without depth
- Generic "full-stack" positioning

> Netflix

> 27 years of experience building web applications...

**After:**

> I help non-technical founders ship AI-powered products without

> - Technical due diligence
