# Comparative Research Synthesis

**Research Date:** 2025-12-06
**Subject:** Jerry Hamlet - Resume and Website Strategy Synthesis
**Purpose:** Actionable recommendations for resume positioning and hamletink.com development

---

## Executive Summary

This synthesis integrates findings from 10 comparative resume analyses, 10 contractor website analyses, LinkedIn optimization research, and the overall strategy guide. The following key findings emerged:

1. **Multi-role identity is essential** - The most successful professionals define themselves with 2-4 distinct but complementary roles (e.g., "Designer, anthropologist, and mediocre developer")

2. **Proof through building trumps credential listing** - Open-source tools, courses, and documented projects establish credibility more effectively than job titles alone

3. **Content-first design dominates** - 9/10 successful websites prioritize clean typography and writing over flashy visuals

4. **AI positioning requires balance** - The most credible AI practitioners acknowledge limitations while demonstrating practical application

5. **The design-engineering hybrid is underexplored in AI consulting** - This represents a significant differentiation opportunity for Jerry

6. **Mission-driven framing resonates** - Leading with "why" and impact rather than job duties creates emotional connection

7. **Teaching establishes authority** - Multiple successful examples leverage educational content (courses, blogs, TILs) as both credibility builders and revenue streams

## Resume Recommendations

### Top 5 Resume Techniques to Adopt

#### 1. Define a Multi-Role Identity

**Action:** Create a memorable identity formula: "[Primary Role], [Secondary Role], and [Unexpected Element]"

**Recommended for Jerry:**

```
Creative Solutions Architect | AI-Augmented Builder | Design-Informed Engineer
```

Or with personality:

```
Solutions Architect, AI practitioner, and recovering designer
```

#### 2. Lead with Mission, Not Job Description

**Action:** Open summary with purpose and impact

**Current Issue:** "20 years experience with the evolving web" is generic
**Recommended Opening:**

```
The intersection of design and engineering is where experiences feel inevitable.
For 27 years, I've built UI systems that bridge both worlds - from AR/VR
interfaces at Meta to streaming experiences serving 100M+ users at Netflix.
Now focused on integrating AI/ML capabilities into modern React applications.
```

#### 3. Curate, Don't List

**Action:** Feature 3-5 high-impact projects rather than exhaustive history

**Recommended Featured Projects:**

1. Meta AR/VR - Gaia Internal Tool (data visualization at scale)
2. Netflix TVUI - Search Experience (30M+ users, functional reactive programming)
3. Netflix TVUI - Build Tools (3+ years evolution, developer experience)
4. Design + Engineering Integration project (demonstrates hybrid approach)
5. AI/ML UI Integration project (when available - demonstrates forward-looking capability)

#### 4. Integrate Personality Authentically

**Action:** Include a distinctive element that makes the resume memorable

**Examples from research:**

- [Wes Bos](https://wesbos.com/): "BBQ Tips"
- [Cassidy Williams](https://cassidoo.co/): "meme-maker"
- [Maggie Appleton](https://maggieappleton.com/): "mediocre developer"

**For Jerry:** Consider what makes you distinctive beyond skills (approach, philosophy, interests)

#### 5. Quantify Everything Possible

**Action:** Add specific metrics to every role

**Metrics to include:**

- Team sizes mentored/led
- User scale (100M+ at Netflix)
- Performance improvements (% reduction in load time, build time)
- Project scope (number of devices, countries, engineers enabled)
- Business impact where provable

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Generic openers** - "20 years experience" tells nothing unique
2. **Listing skills without context** - Show skills in action through project descriptions
3. **Exhaustive history** - More is not better; curate for impact
4. **Missing AI/ML keywords** - Current market demands this positioning
5. **Underselling design background** - This is a key differentiator at senior level
6. **Using outdated company names** - "Facebook" should be "Meta"
7. **Claiming breadth without depth proof** - "Creative Solutions Architect" needs concrete examples

## Website Recommendations

### Top 5 Website Features to Implement

#### 1. Content-Forward Design with Personality

**Action:** Prioritize clean typography and writing, but add distinctive visual elements

**Implementation:**

- Minimalist aesthetic (9/10 successful sites use this)
- Dark/light mode toggle (table stakes for technical audiences)
- 1-2 accent colors for brand recognition
- Custom illustrations or interactive elements showcasing frontend expertise
- Consider hover animations on SVGs ([Maggie Appleton](https://maggieappleton.com/) approach)

#### 2. Clear Value Proposition and "Start Here" Section

**Action:** Guide new visitors immediately to understand positioning

**Implementation:**

- Hero with clear statement of who you serve and what you do
- "Start Here" or "New Here?" section ([Eugene Yan](https://eugeneyan.com/) model)
- Three-column service overview below hero
- Avoid aggressive CTAs; use soft, permission-based engagement

#### 3. Case Studies as Proof

**Action:** Develop 3-5 detailed case studies following a structured framework

**Case Study Structure:**

1. Overview (client, timeline, role, technologies, one-sentence result)
2. Challenge (business context, technical constraints)
3. Approach (decisions and rationale)
4. Solution (architecture, key innovations)
5. Results (quantified outcomes, business impact)
6. Learnings (what worked, what you'd do differently)

**Recommended Case Studies for hamletink.com:**

1. Meta AR/VR - Gaia Internal Tool
2. Netflix TVUI - Search Experience
3. Netflix TVUI - Build Tools
4. Design + Engineering Integration (composite/philosophical)
5. AI/ML UI Integration (when project available)

#### 4. Multi-Path Engagement

**Action:** Provide multiple entry points for different visitor types

**Implementation:**

- Newsletter signup (soft CTA, builds ongoing relationship)
- "Schedule a Consultation" (primary conversion for consulting)
- Resume/capability deck download
- Blog/insights section for thought leadership
- Social links for async engagement

#### 5. Proof Through Building (Open Source or Tools)

**Action:** Create or highlight tools that demonstrate capability

**Options:**

- Open source contributions on GitHub
- Small utility tools that showcase skills
- Interactive tutorials or demos
- TIL (Today I Learned) section for continuous learning documentation

### Differentiation Opportunities

Based on the research, these areas are underexplored in the consultant landscape:

1. **AI Agentic Development Focus** - Few consultants explicitly position around agentic systems
2. **Design-Engineering Hybrid in AI Consulting** - The [Maggie Appleton](https://maggieappleton.com/)/[Brittany Chiang](https://brittanychiang.com/) intersection is rare in AI consulting
3. **Creative Solutions Framing** - Most consultants position technically; creative framing differentiates
4. **Client Portal/Dashboard** - None of the analyzed sites offer this - could be unique value-add

### Visual Design Direction

#### Design System Recommendations:

**Color Palette:**

- Primary: Deep, sophisticated tone (navy, charcoal, or dark teal)
- Accent: Vibrant but professional (coral, teal, or amber)
- Neutral: Clean whites and light grays
- Consider dark mode as default (7/10 sites offer toggle, many default to dark)

**Typography:**

- Headlines: Modern geometric sans-serif (Inter, Satoshi)
- Body: Highly readable sans-serif (system fonts for performance)
- Code: Monospace for technical credibility (JetBrains Mono, Fira Code)
- Consider serif + sans-serif pairing for visual hierarchy ([Maggie Appleton](https://maggieappleton.com/) approach)

**Inspiration Sources:**

- **[Maggie Appleton](https://maggieappleton.com/)** - Custom SVG illustrations, hover animations, cream/crimson palette
- **[Josh Comeau](https://www.joshwcomeau.com/)** - Animated SVG backgrounds, playful personality, dark mode
- **[Brittany Chiang](https://brittanychiang.com/)** - Polished professional aesthetic, Easter eggs, smooth transitions
- **[Eugene Yan](https://eugeneyan.com/)** - Content metrics display, clear "Start Here" onboarding

### Pricing Considerations (Research Gap)

The research identified that most consultant sites do not display pricing. Common approaches observed:

1. **No public pricing** - "Schedule a consultation" (most common)
2. **Starting rates** - "Projects starting at $X"
3. **Day rates** - For advisory/consulting work
4. **Course pricing** - For educational products

**Recommendation:** Start with consultation-based approach without public pricing. Develop pricing tiers based on engagement type (hourly advisory vs. project-based vs. retainer).

## Questions for User

### Positioning Clarifications

1. **Identity Balance:** How to balance "Creative Solutions Architect" breadth with specific expertise areas? The most memorable positioning from research was narrow (accessibility, AI tools) not broad. What 1-2 areas should be primary focus?

2. **AI Positioning Depth:** Is the goal to position as AI-native builder (like [Willison](https://simonwillison.net/), creating tools) or AI-enabled creative (using AI as tool)? These require different evidence.

3. **Teaching Component:** Would creating educational content (courses, workshops, blog posts) strengthen positioning? Multiple research examples leveraged teaching for authority. Is this of interest?

4. **Design Credential Substantiation:** How to best demonstrate "design-informed" claim? Research examples had formal design background or design-focused projects. What evidence exists for Jerry's design expertise?

5. **Personality Element:** What makes Jerry distinctive beyond technical skills? The research showed personality integration (BBQ tips, meme-making, "mediocre developer" humility) creates memorable positioning.

### Service Offering Decisions

6. **Primary Client Target:** Startups needing senior architecture, agencies seeking specialists, or enterprises building AI experiences? Messaging differs significantly.

7. **Engagement Models:** What engagement types are preferred?
   - Project-based (defined scope)
   - Retainer (ongoing advisory)
   - Fractional (part-time principal engineer)
   - Training/workshops

8. **Rate Range:** What is the target rate for consulting work? (Not for public display, but to inform positioning and client targeting)

9. **Geographic Scope:** Local Bay Area only, US-based, or global remote work?

10. **Content Investment:** How much time is available for content creation (blog posts, TILs, open source)? This significantly impacts positioning strategy.

### Design Direction Preferences

11. **Dark vs. Light Mode:** Should dark mode be default (common for technical audiences) or light mode?

12. **Visual Investment:** How much custom illustration or design work is appropriate? Options range from minimal (text-focused like [Simon Willison](https://simonwillison.net/)) to highly visual ([Maggie Appleton](https://maggieappleton.com/) custom SVGs).

13. **Personality Level:** How much personality/playfulness is appropriate for target clients?

14. **Photography:** Is there a professional photo available for use? Sites with photos generally perform better for personal consulting brands.

### Feature Prioritization

15. **MVP Features:** For initial launch, which are essential?
    - [ ] Homepage with value proposition
    - [ ] About page with full bio
    - [ ] Services page with offerings
    - [ ] Case studies (how many?)
    - [ ] Blog/insights section
    - [ ] Contact form
    - [ ] Newsletter signup
    - [ ] Resume/PDF download

16. **Blog Strategy:** Start with curated posts on high-value topics, or TIL-style for volume? [Swyx](https://www.swyx.io/) and [Willison](https://simonwillison.net/) models differ here.

17. **Case Study Depth:** Full detailed case studies or project summaries initially? Full case studies require more effort but provide better proof.

### Technical Decisions

18. **Framework Preference:** Astro (recommended in strategy) or Next.js (demonstrates React expertise)?

19. **Newsletter Platform:** Buttondown, ConvertKit, Substack, or other?

20. **Contact Form Handling:** Simple form (Formspree, Netlify Forms) or integration with scheduling (Calendly)?
