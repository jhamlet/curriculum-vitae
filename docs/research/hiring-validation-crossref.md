## Consensus Findings

All three personas converge on these evaluation criteria:

### 1. Time is the Scarcest Resource

| Persona          | Initial Screen Time | Key Implication                   |
| ---------------- | ------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Startup Founder  | 5-15 minutes        | Pattern recognition, gut feel     |
| Engineering HM   | 7-11 seconds        | Eye-tracking data, ATS dependency |
| Strategic Leader | 30 sec - 2 min      | More attention, but still fast    |

**Consensus:** Front-load critical information. The first 3-5 lines
carry disproportionate weight regardless of audience.

### 2. Outcomes Over Activities

All three personas explicitly devalue activity metrics ("managed team of
8", "used 12 frameworks") in favor of outcome metrics ("reduced latency
by 40%", "increased conversion by 15%").

- Founder: "Problem orientation: Do they talk about problems solved or
  technologies used?"
- Eng HM: Tier 1 (business outcomes) beats Tier 4 (activity metrics)
- Strategic: "Technology decisions connected to revenue, market
  expansion, or organizational transformation"

### 3. Contract-to-Hire as De-Risking Strategy

All three personas view trial periods favorably:

- Founder: "Preferred de-risking strategy" (2-12 weeks)
- Eng HM: "Strong deliverables and long engagements counter [commitment
  bias]"
- Strategic: "Reduces risk of costly bad hires" (3-6 months typical)

**Consensus:** Contract positioning is an advantage, not a liability,
when framed correctly.

### 4. Red Flags Are Disqualifying Faster Than Green Flags Qualify

All personas identify similar disqualifiers:

- Job hopping without narrative
- Vague scope descriptions
- Technology lists without depth
- "Too corporate" behavioral signals
- Speaking negatively about previous employers

### 5. Communication Quality as Proxy for Capability

All three evaluate communication as a signal:

- Founder: "Communication clarity: Can they explain complex things
  simply?"
- Eng HM: "Jargon-heavy explanations suggest shallow understanding"
- Strategic: "Resume itself demonstrates communication skills"

## Divergent Perspectives

### 1. Technical Depth vs Breadth

| Persona         | Position                               | Implication for Jerry               |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Startup Founder | Breadth valued - "figure it out" types | Emphasize cross-domain capability   |
| Engineering HM  | Depth for IC, breadth for leadership   | Position as Staff+ (needs both)     |
| Strategic       | Breadth of organizational impact       | Emphasize systems thinking at scale |

**Why This Matters:** Jerry's positioning as a cross-cutting problem
solver aligns better with Founder and Strategic perspectives.
Engineering HM may want more depth evidence in specific domains.

**Resolution:** Lead with breadth (cross-domain impact), but ensure
depth proof points are accessible (Netflix global architecture, Meta
AR/VR systems).

### 2. FAANG Experience Interpretation

| Persona         | View of FAANG Background                                   |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Startup Founder | "Proof of principles, not pedigree" - shows scale thinking |
| Engineering HM  | Creates shortcuts in screening - recognized brand          |
| Strategic       | "Builders vs operators" - FAANG can signal operator        |

**Why This Matters:** FAANG experience is a double-edged sword.
Strategic leaders may view it as "operator" evidence. Startup founders
want proof of startup compatibility.

**Resolution:** Frame FAANG experience as:

- Netflix: Building (1 to 212 countries) not operating
- Meta: Internal tools that enabled others (builder mindset)
- Emphasize autonomy, ambiguity tolerance, shipping velocity

### 3. Timeline Expectations

| Persona         | Typical Hiring Timeline | Contract Decision |
| --------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------- |
| Startup Founder | 2-4 weeks               | 1-2 weeks         |
| Engineering HM  | 2-6 weeks               | Not specified     |
| Strategic       | 130 days (executive)    | 3-6 months trial  |

**Why This Matters:** Different personas operate on vastly different
timelines. Strategic hires may take months; startup founder hires happen
in days.

**Resolution:** Jerry's target market (Series A-B) aligns with faster
timelines. Position for rapid engagement while demonstrating strategic
capability.

### 4. What "Senior" Means

| Persona         | Definition of Senior                                        |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Startup Founder | Works independently, identifies problems, knows when to ask |
| Engineering HM  | Depth in specialization, can mentor, architectural thinking |
| Strategic       | Influence without authority, cross-team impact, builder     |

**Why This Matters:** Same title, different expectations. Jerry must
signal the right flavor of "senior" for each audience.

**Resolution:** The "Creative Solutions Engineer" positioning threads
the needle - emphasizes problem identification and independence (Founder
appeal), architectural thinking (HM appeal), and cross-team impact
(Strategic appeal).

## Gaps Identified

### 1. AI/ML Evaluation Criteria

**Gap:** None of the three research documents address how evaluators
specifically assess AI/ML capabilities or AI-augmented development
skills.

**Risk:** This is a novel space. Evaluators may not have established
patterns for assessing AI-native approaches.

**Mitigation Needed:**

- Research current AI/ML hiring practices specifically
- Identify how to demonstrate spec-driven development methodology
- Determine whether AI skills are evaluated as technical depth or as
  process innovation

### 2. Remote/Async-First Evaluation

**Gap:** Research assumes traditional interview processes. Limited
discussion of how remote-first or async-first evaluations differ.

**Risk:** Jerry's non-negotiable for remote-first may encounter friction
with evaluators who prefer in-person assessment.

**Mitigation Needed:**

- Research remote hiring practices at target companies
- Identify how to demonstrate collaboration capability without in-person
  interaction

### 3. Developer Tools Industry Specifics

**Gap:** Research is general to tech hiring. No specific patterns for
developer tools/DevOps/DX space.

**Risk:** Developer tools companies may have unique evaluation criteria
(e.g., valuing open source contributions more heavily, expecting
specific platform familiarity).

**Mitigation Needed:**

- Survey job postings from companies like Vercel, Railway, Linear,
  Fly.io
- Identify industry-specific signals and vocabulary

### 4. Fractional/Advisory Positioning

**Gap:** Research covers contract-to-hire but not fractional leadership
or advisory engagements specifically.

**Risk:** The $250-350/hr fractional leadership tier may require
different positioning than the $175-200/hr contract tier.

**Mitigation Needed:**

- Research fractional CTO/VP Engineering market specifically
- Identify how advisory relationships are initiated and evaluated

### 5. Age/Experience Perception

**Gap:** Limited discussion of how 27+ years experience is perceived
across personas.

**Risk:** Author profile explicitly flags ageism concern ("Does it lead
with years in a way that risks ageism?").

**Research suggests mixed signals:**

- Founder: Tenure patterns matter less than tenure stories
- Eng HM: Early career can be compressed
- Strategic: Experience valued for strategic roles

**Mitigation Needed:**

- Research current perception of senior/experienced candidates in
  startup space
- Identify language that signals wisdom without triggering age
  assumptions

## Contradictions to Resolve

### 1. Technology Lists: Show Them or Not?

**Contradiction:**

- Eng HM: "Missing keywords that match job requirements" causes
  automatic rejection (ATS)
- Eng HM: "Listing 30+ technologies with no depth indicators" is a red
  flag
- Founder: Technology lists without problem context are weak

**Resolution:** Include technology keywords for ATS matching, but
subordinate them to outcome statements. The skills section exists; it
just shouldn't lead.

### 2. Tenure Narrative for Contract Work

**Contradiction:**

- Multiple short stints are a job-hopping red flag
- Contract work is explicitly shorter by nature
- Must differentiate intentional contract work from involuntary job
  hopping

**Resolution:** Professional framing ("Independent Consultant" not
"Freelancer") and explicit contract designation on resume. Show long
engagements where applicable.

### 3. Leading with Experience vs Hiding It

**Contradiction:**

- Strategic personas value experience and long tenure
- Author profile warns against leading with years (ageism risk)
- Startup founders care about behavior patterns, not credentials

**Resolution:** Lead with capability and outcomes, not years. Let tenure
be discoverable rather than headline. "27 years bridging design and
engineering" becomes "career arc from design foundation through
principal leadership" - the years are implied, not stated.

### 4. Proof Gap for AI Work

**Contradiction:**

- Founders want evidence, not claims
- AI-augmented approach is recent (mid-November 2025)
- Traditional proof (shipped products, GitHub, etc.) may not exist yet

**Research doesn't fully address** how to handle legitimate capability
gaps.

**Resolution Approach:**

- Process documentation (spec-driven methodology) as proof of approach
- This resume improvement process itself as demonstration
- Position as "early adopter ahead of curve" rather than "expert"

## Recommendations

### For Persona Refinement

1. **Add AI/ML Evaluation Module:** Research how evaluators assess AI
   capabilities specifically. Current personas lack this dimension.

2. **Add Remote-First Evaluation Patterns:** Research how async-first
   companies evaluate candidates. Current research assumes traditional
   process.

3. **Add Developer Tools Industry Specifics:** Survey actual job
   postings from target companies (Vercel, Railway, Linear, etc.) to
   identify industry-specific vocabulary and expectations.

4. **Add Fractional Leadership Perspective:** Research fractional CTO/VP
   market to support the higher rate tier ($250-350/hr).

### For Resume Strategy

1. **Front-load Ruthlessly:** All personas agree first impression
   carries disproportionate weight. The positioning statement must
   appear in first 3 lines.

2. **Outcome-First, Technology-Second:** Every bullet should lead with
   impact, with technology as supporting detail.

3. **Builder Language Explicitly:** Use "built," "established,"
   "created" language to signal builder identity over operator identity.

4. **Address FAANG Transition:** Explicitly frame Netflix/Meta as
   building experience, not operating experience.

5. **Professional Contract Framing:** Use "Independent Consultant" or
   "Principal Consultant" positioning with clear contract designations.

6. **AI Proof Strategy:** Develop visible artifacts of AI-augmented work
   (process documentation, methodology writeups, portfolio pieces)
   before market entry.

### For Market Entry

1. **Validate Rate Against Current Market:** Research current Series A-B
   contract rates for senior/staff engineers. $175-200/hr may need
   justification.

2. **Build Proof Points Before Q1 2026:** The AI proof gap is real.
   Prioritize visible work between now and market entry.

3. **Develop "Why Not Full-Time" Narrative:** All personas show some
   bias toward full-time. Have clear, confident answer ready.

4. **Prepare 100-Day Plan Framework:** Strategic persona research shows
   this is common for senior roles. Having a framework ready
   demonstrates strategic thinking.
