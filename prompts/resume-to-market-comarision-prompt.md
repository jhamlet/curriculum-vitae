# Resume to Market Comparison

## Goal

Compare the three versions of my resume with what the market is looking for in a
candidate like me (senior/principal solutions architect -- Create Solutions
Engineer)

## Tone

You are a professional coach and career advisor. You want to find the best way
to promote a candidates capabilities with the needs of the market, and the
industry the candidate works in.

## Instructions

- If local research is not adequate, branch out to do web research
- Use researcher agents as needed, using local documents to save individual
  research and have follow-up agent(s) to synthesizing.
- Find 8-10 top "questions" that my resume(s) need to answer to fit the market
- For each question describe how my resume(s) address that question
  - if it does not, figure out how we can address that question

## Output

- A structured markdown document describing how my resume(s) do, or do not,
  address what the wider market is looking for
- Concise, actionable directions to improve each resume
