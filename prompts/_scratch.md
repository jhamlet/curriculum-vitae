# Resume and Contractor Comparison

## Goal

Gather 10 comparative resumes, and 10 comparative contractor sites. The point is
to find comparative resumes and websites to measure, and guide, the development
of my resume and contracting services.

## Instructions

- Use one, or more, researcher agents to do the research.
- Use a final researcher agent to cross-reference the research into a summary
  document
- DO NOT assume my past experience is the experience I want to focus on
- I want to focus on providing creative solutions with my experience
- Add to my experience as a fluent AI agentic developer
- Use my understanding of design and full-featured solution finding as a
  differentiator
- For samples store a URL for websites
  - I want to have sample website designs to draw inspiration from
  - Also note differentiated offerings (newsletters, client logins, etc...)
- If questions arise during search, note those so the final researcher can ask
  user questions for follow-up research

## Output

- Compile individual research into sub-director(y|ies) in docs/research/
- Put summary findings in docs/research
