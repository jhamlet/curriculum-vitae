# Market Research Prompt

## Goal

Gather research on what the market is looking for in someone with my
capabilities. I want to tailor my job search and contractor advertisement to
what employers and businesses are looking for. I want to compile the information
on the market that correlates with my skill set, and the work I want to do.

## Instructions

- Use researcher agents to fetch and collate the information
- Use a separate agent to synthesize the research and provide a concise,
  targeted report on how to best write my resume

## Output

- A well formatted markdown document in docs/guides/
