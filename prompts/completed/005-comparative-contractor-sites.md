<research_objective>
Find and analyze 10 comparative contractor/consulting websites from professionals who position themselves as creative solution providers, AI specialists, or design-engineering consultants. These examples will guide the development of hamletink.com, emphasizing creative solutions and AI fluency.

This research is for Jerry Hamlet's contracting website, which should differentiate through creative problem-solving, AI agentic development expertise, and design-informed engineering.
</research_objective>

<positioning_context>

- Design + Engineering hybrid consultant

**Website features to note:**
- How they present their value proposition
- Differentiated offerings (newsletters, client portals, tools, etc.)
- Visual design and branding approaches
- Call-to-action strategies
- Case study presentation
- Pricing/engagement models shown (or hidden)

<search_criteria>
Find contractor/consulting websites from:
1. **AI/ML consultants** - Specializing in LLM integration, agentic systems
2. **Creative technologists** - Bridging design and engineering
3. **Design engineers/studios** - Technical consulting with design focus
4. **Independent principal engineers** - High-end technical consulting
5. **Boutique agencies (1-3 person)** - Solo or small team positioning

**Look for:**
- Single-person or small team consulting sites
- Sites that emphasize AI/ML/LLM expertise
- Creative or design-forward presentations
- Clear service offerings and positioning
- Unique features (tools, resources, client areas)

**Avoid:**
- Large agency websites
- Pure design agencies (no engineering)
- Commodity development shops
- Sites without clear positioning
</search_criteria>
