# Pattern: Gap Explanation

## When to Apply

- Resume has employment gap > 6 months
- Current explanation is vague or missing
- Reviewer feedback flags the gap as concern

## Approach

1. Replace vague language with specific activities
2. Include concrete deliverables or learning outcomes
3. Connect gap activities to target role relevance
4. Add external validation if available (OSS, consulting, etc.)

## Example Transformations

**Vague:**

> Took time to explore new technologies and the evolving development
> landscape.

**Specific:**

> Built multi-agent orchestration systems and spec-driven workflows,
> contributing to open-source AI tooling projects. Developed
> production-ready methodology now shipping in developer tools.

---

**Vague:**

> Transitioned from hands-on development to exploring modern practices.

**Specific:**

> Since 2022, developed AI-augmented development methodology through
> independent research and open-source contributions. Built automation
> systems and spec-driven workflows now shipping in production tools.

## Trade-offs

| Gain                                | Cost                                  |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Addresses red flag directly         | May invite follow-up questions        |
| Shows continuous skill development  | Requires concrete evidence to back up |
| Demonstrates initiative/self-driven | Balance specificity with brevity      |
| Connects gap to role relevance      | Over-explanation can seem defensive   |
